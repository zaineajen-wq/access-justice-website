About

Meet Eugenia

Access Justice Paralegal Services was created around a simple idea: legal help should be easier to understand, easier to access and tailored to what a client actually needs.

The practice is being designed as a virtual-first service with defined scope, transparent pricing and practical support for people who may not need full representation.

In-person appointments in St. Catharines will be available by prior arrangement at a private meeting location.
