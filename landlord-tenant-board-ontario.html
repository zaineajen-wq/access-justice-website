How we can help

Discuss your tenancy matter

A consultation can help you understand your options. The consultation and written assessment package starts at $150 plus HST.

Prepare a notice or application

Ask about N4 notices and L1 or L9 applications. Tenants can inquire about T1 applications and individually scoped help with T2, T5 or T6 applications.

Prepare for your next step

Ask about document review, hearing preparation, motions or review requests. Hearing attendance is separately scoped and quoted if available.

Start with an initial inquiry

Services are subject to a conflict and suitability check. Please keep confidential documents and detailed sensitive information out of the initial inquiry. Sending an inquiry does not create a paralegal-client relationship.
