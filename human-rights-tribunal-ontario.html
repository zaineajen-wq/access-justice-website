VIRTUAL PARALEGAL SERVICES ACROSS ONTARIO

Human Rights Tribunal of Ontario Paralegal Services

Access Justice reviews inquiries about eligible Human Rights Tribunal of Ontario (HRTO) matters and offers virtual, limited-scope paralegal assistance across Ontario.

Open intake form

How we can help

Discuss your matter

Tell us whether you are considering an HRTO application, responding to one, or looking for help with an existing matter.

Ask about a specific task

Depending on the matter, ask about a focused consultation, document review or help preparing an agreed document.

Confirm scope and fees

We assess suitability and conflicts before confirming the available service, its scope and the fee in writing.

Start with an initial inquiry

Services are subject to a conflict and suitability check. Please keep confidential documents and detailed sensitive information out of the initial inquiry. Sending an inquiry does not create a paralegal-client relationship.
